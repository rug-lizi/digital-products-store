## 回答3
问题: Can you sell digital products from China?

Yes, but it's complicated. Here's what I learned the hard way:

What DOESN'T work:
- Gumroad: blocked by GFW
- Lemon Squeezy / Ko-fi / Payhip: all use Stripe, which doesn't support China merchants
- PayPal: might work if your account is in good standing (mine was permanently banned)

What DOES work:
- Self-hosted store + Stripe Hong Kong account (requires HK bank account + HK address + Chinese passport)
- Amazon KDP + Payoneer
- Google Play + Payoneer

The key: you need a bank account in a Stripe-supported country. For me, that was a招商银行香港分行 account. Stripe HK accepts Chinese passport holders.

## 回答5
问题: How do I set up Stripe if my country is not supported?

If you're in a country Stripe doesn't support (like mainland China), here's the workaround I used:

1. Open a bank account in a Stripe-supported country. I used招商银行香港分行 (CMB Hong Kong branch). You need to visit HK once or use their online application if eligible.

2. Register Stripe with:
   - Country: Hong Kong
   - Business type: Individual
   - Name: Your real name (on passport)
   - ID: Chinese passport (accepted by Stripe HK)
   - Address: A Hong Kong address (you need a real one — use a friend's or a virtual office)
   - Bank account: Your HK bank account for payouts

3. Stripe will verify your identity. With a Chinese passport + HK address + HK bank, this passes.

4. Now you can accept card payments globally.

Important: Don't try to fake your country. Stripe KYC verifies your actual identity documents. Using a VPN won't help — they check your ID, not your IP.
